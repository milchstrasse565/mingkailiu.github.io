About Me

Mingkai Liu

I am a second-year master's student at Peking University, majoring in Integrated Circuit Engineering.

My research interests lie in 3D computer vision and deep learning, with a focus on 3D reconstruction, SLAM, scene localization, and robotics. I am interested in building efficient visual systems that connect geometric understanding with real-world action.
